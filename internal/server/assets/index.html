File transfers coming in a later stage

This is the Flitnix phone page. Sending and receiving files between your phone and your desktop will be enabled in an upcoming version. You are connected to the Flitnix server on your computer, but no transfer endpoints are available yet.

How it will work

  • Open this page from your phone's browser on your local network.
  • Pair with the desktop app using a QR code.
  • Send or receive files in either direction, streamed directly over your local connection — no cloud, no accounts.

Flitnix runs entirely on your local network.
